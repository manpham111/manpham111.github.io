Case Study — Pavilion Design Journey

1. Concept Genesis (Week 1)

Image Placeholder — Concept Sketch

Early hand sketches established the pavilion's identity: openness, daylight, and communal flow. AI tools evolved these sketches into concept images exploring massing, materiality, and evening activation.

2. Diagram Logic & Massing (Week 2)

Image Placeholder — Diagrams

Circulation, daylight, noise, and program diagrams clarified the project's intent. AI-assisted massing studies carved a courtyard void and organized residential wings.

3. Elevation & Materiality (Week 2)

Image Placeholder — Elevations

Façade rhythm, glazing proportions, and shading strategies were refined. A curated material palette blended concrete, fiber-cement, wood, and perforated metal.

4. Custom AI Tool Concept (Week 3)

A conceptual AI tool was proposed to translate ambiguous sketches into structured massing models using site data and program requirements.

5. Parametric + AI Strategy (Week 4)

Rule-based geometry ensured responsiveness to client changes while preserving narrative coherence. AI tested façade variations and environmental logic.

6. Contextual Rendering Variations (Week 5)

Image Placeholder — Render Variations

Generative Fill added diversity, seasonal shifts, and environmental context to a base render.

7. Technical Documentation Layer (Week 5)

SketchPro AI automated SD → CD sheet generation, annotation, scheduling, and QA/QC.

8. Cinematic Pavilion Animation (Week 6)

Image Placeholder — Animation Still

A 60-second animation communicated spatial experience through curated camera paths, lighting, and pacing.

9. Construction Strategy & Administration (Week 6)

A full construction proposal included phasing, schedule, cost estimate, risk log, site observation report, and benchmarking memo.

10. Business Strategy & Proposal (Week 7)

The NEXUS Pavilion proposal defined market positioning, target clients, value proposition, architectural concept, and an executive summary.

11. Design Development (Week 8)

Geometry, materiality, and spatial programming were refined using AI-assisted optimization.

12. Advanced Rendering (Week 9)

Image Placeholder — Final Render

Exterior and interior renders were produced with AI-assisted lighting and post-processing.

13. Final Drawings (Week 10)

Plans, sections, and elevations were finalized with automated annotation and tagging.

14. Final Model + QA/QC (Week 11)

The final model underwent clash detection, schedule validation, and sheet set review.

15. Final Presentation (Week 12)

Image Placeholder — Presentation Board

Final boards and animation were assembled into a cohesive presentation package.
